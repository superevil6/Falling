RULES
  Every tile is 256 x 256 px and repeats sideways with itself.
  The tiles of a layer stack vertically in ANY order, any number of times - cycle or shuffle them.
  Draw farther layers behind nearer ones and scroll them slower (the suggested speeds above).
  Drawn at 64 art px and exported at 4x by nearest-neighbour. Use Nearest filtering.
